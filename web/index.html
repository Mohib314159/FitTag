Measurement options +

Supply an actual lens-to-floor distance. Hold the camera parallel to the floor. This mode uses geometry, without a depth model.

Check with a second photo +

Take another photo of the same item. This can catch a wrong estimate, but matching results can still be wrong.

Optional. Both photos are uploaded only when you tap Read photo.

Camera settings +

We use 35 mm equivalent focal length from the photo when available. Otherwise we assume an 84° diagonal field of view. That assumption can change every centimetre result.

No ruler in the photo. Check the estimated size before using it.

Photo tips +
  1. 01
    Show every edge.

    Waistband at the top. Separate the legs and include both hems.

  2. 02
    Leave floor around it.

    A plain contrasting surface, even light. Keep some floor visible around the clothes.

  3. 03
    Use the normal 1× camera.

    Shoot parallel to the floor. Avoid portrait mode, digital zoom and cropped screenshots.

    About these estimates +

    A photo alone cannot tell us the exact size of an item. FitTag estimates distance to suggest measurements. Those estimates can be far off.

    Check the lines, then compare with a tape measure or enter one measurement you know. A second photo can catch some mistakes, but it cannot prove the size is correct.

    The example is a computer-made image with a known size. It shows where the size estimate goes wrong. It is not evidence of accuracy on real clothes.

    Three quick steps

    1. Lay the clothes flat. Use a plain surface and the normal 1× camera.
    2. Take a photo or choose one. Keep every edge in the frame.
    3. Check the lines. Drag their ends to adjust them. Check the size with a tape, or enter a measurement you already know.
