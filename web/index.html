How it works

A ruler the camera understands.

No app to wrestle with — the seller already lays clothes flat to photograph them. They just lay it on the FitTag sheet.

STEP 01

Drop in the tag

The garment sits on a printed sheet with corner markers — a scale the lens can read, even at an angle.

STEP 02

Geometry measures it

One photo is rectified to a true top-down view, and the silhouette is measured to the centimetre, with error bars. Nothing is estimated.

STEP 03

The buyer sees the fit

Buyers enter their measurements once — or match a garment they own — and see exactly how it'll sit, per zone.

    Why this can't be copied

    Everyone else needs data a one-off item doesn't have.

    Shows a plausible image — and its own app shrinks the clothes to match your photo. Size-blind.

    Works off a million customers' return data per item. Secondhand has no SKU and no returns.
